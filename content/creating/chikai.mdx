Chikai is a sake brand positioning itself in the European premium spirits market — a category where the visual language has been dominated by wine and whisky for decades, and where sake has often defaulted to generic Japanese aesthetic signifiers without much interrogation.

The brief was to create something contemporary and European-facing while remaining honest about the product's provenance and craft.

The identity drew on the tension between traditional craft and modern restraint. Typography in both Latin and Japanese characters had to sit in harmony — a technical challenge as much as an aesthetic one. The colour palette moved away from the reds and golds that dominate the category toward something cooler and more considered.

![Chikai brand and packaging exploration](/images/creating/chikai/detail-01.webp)

The web design extended the brand system into a product-focused experience: short on copy, long on atmosphere. The work needed to earn trust for a product that many European drinkers were encountering seriously for the first time.
