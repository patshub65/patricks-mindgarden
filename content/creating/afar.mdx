Some briefs arrive as paragraphs of requirements. This one arrived as a playlist and a mood board.

AFAR is a Berlin-based artist. The artwork needed to capture something about the music — which occupies that particular territory between ambient and electronic, meditative and rhythmic. Nothing too obvious. Nothing that would look like a genre signifier.

The artwork system was built around a restricted palette and typographic decisions that did more work than decoration usually does. The poster had to live at A0 in a venue and as a 3×3 centimetre Instagram preview — the mark of a good piece of print design is that both feel intentional.

The album sleeve, the vinyl label, and the tour poster shared a visual language without being identical. Coherent without being repetitive — each had its own problem to solve.
