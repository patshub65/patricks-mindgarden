The name does a lot of work. *Bananas Are Berries* is a podcast about counterintuitive facts, hidden complexity, and the pleasure of being wrong about something you thought you understood. The identity needed to carry that sensibility — playful, curious, slightly wrong-footed by design.

The mark leans into the botanical absurdity of the name: visual elements that mix the expected with the technically-accurate-but-surprising. Colour choices that are warm without being naive. A typographic voice that feels both editorial and approachable.

![Bananas Are Berries logo and artwork system](/images/creating/bananas-are-berries/detail-01.png)

The identity extended to podcast artwork, episode templates, and a small suite of social assets. Podcast branding has its own constraints — it needs to be immediately legible at very small sizes across dark and light backgrounds, in contexts where it's competing with dozens of other thumbnails for attention.

![Bananas Are Berries social and podcast artwork](/images/creating/bananas-are-berries/detail-02.jpg)

The result is a brand that earns the name.
