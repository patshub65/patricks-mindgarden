Stetig & Wandel is an initiative for digital agencies and firms in Germany — built around the idea that competing companies can do better work by sharing knowledge, principles, and a set of values rather than guarding their edges against each other.

The old brand read like a placeholder. The organisation had matured past it. Our team at Jut-so led the brand refresh and website redesign.

## Brand

We began with a discovery phase: workshops to understand what Stetig & Wandel actually stood for beyond the tagline, audience benchmarking, and an honest assessment of where the existing brand failed. The brief that emerged was to evolve the identity rather than replace it — maintaining the recognition they'd built while making it feel like it meant something.

The visual outcome: a grey/black foundation with bold accent colour. Typographically confident and clean for display use. We sourced and worked with an illustrator to develop custom iconography — the kind of detail that separates a real brand from a template.

The core visual motif was the double-ampersand: *Stetig && Wandel* — steady and change, two things that aren't opposites if you're paying attention.

## Website

The website redesign was approached with the kind of rigour a multi-disciplinary team actually allows. We restructured the sitemap to make the initiative's value proposition clearer on arrival, then moved through wireframes, responsive design, and visual implementation together.

Key goal: the content needed to serve two audiences simultaneously — agencies considering joining, and potential collaborators or clients trying to understand what the network stood for. The design used the brand's two-register system (functional editorial type for body, expressive display for emphasis) to carry both without collapse.

The custom illustrations landed throughout the site as a through-line — making the brand feel made, not assembled.

## Outcome

The redesigned Stetig & Wandel site presented a version of the initiative that matched what the organisation had become. Clear, warm, distinctly not-corporate. The collaborators at the centre of the project — designers, illustrators, developers — all working in the same direction is exactly what Stetig & Wandel is supposed to be about.
