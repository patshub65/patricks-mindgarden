Loominate was a Berlin design studio — and the team behind several of the larger projects in this portfolio. Building the studio's own identity was one of the more interesting briefs, because you know the client unreasonably well.

The name carries the double meaning that most brand names aspire to: illumination and the loom — craft, weave, structure. The identity needed to hold both without explaining itself too hard.

The visual system was built for a design-forward audience. Clean without being cold, typographically precise, with a colour approach that could flex across client contexts without losing its own character. A design studio's brand needs to say *we know what we're doing* while leaving enough room for the work itself to be the loudest thing in the room.

![Loominate visual identity preview](/images/creating/loominate/detail-01.jpg)

The system covered logo, typography, colour, motion principles, and presentation templates — the full kit for a studio that was regularly producing work across web, print, and screen.
