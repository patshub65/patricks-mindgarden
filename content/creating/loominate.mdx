As co-founder of Loominate, a Berlin-based creative agency, I led the initiative to evolve our brand and business model. The challenge was one every growing agency eventually faces: we had built something capable of doing almost anything, which made it harder to clearly communicate what we actually stood for.

The goal was to move from a do-it-all offering — comprehensive but complicated — toward something more deliberate: a focused agency with select, packaged services that clients could understand and trust quickly.

![Loominate visual identity preview](/images/creating/loominate/detail-01.jpg)

I wore many hats across the project, as you do when it's your own company. My main contributions were leading the rebranding effort and setting the overall creative direction — from how we articulated the pivot in positioning and messaging, to how that translated visually across the brand system and website.

The website redesign was a key deliverable: it needed to reflect the sharper positioning without losing the warmth and credibility we'd built. Less explanation, more conviction.
